This portfolio piece is an improvement / extensions of the model reported in this poster: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1nnV-cHYAQVcc5l6ge7PP3fHaVXDlSpSQ/view?usp=sharing.

What’s going on is to find out how promximal minority stressors, such as internalized homophobia and lack of LGBTQ+ affirmation, would predict distal stressors–discrimination through behavioral enactment of LGBTQ+ identity–acts of coming out.

A critique of the model in the poster is using the count of specific coming-out strategies participants used in each of the five categories as mediators. Using counts of specific strategies is problematic for two reasons. First, participants were given more specific strategies to choose from in some categories and participants were given fewer specific strategies to choose from in other categories. Therefore, the scales of the counts are not uniform, which makes it harder to understand the coefficients. Second, using counts introduces a methodological confound. After indicating they had used strategies of a category, participants were given a given a list of specific strategies under a category and they reported the specific strategies they had used. Then, participants were asked a series of follow-up questions regarding each specific strategy they had used, such as whether they were comforable with using it. After this, participants were given a list of specific strategies of another category. Therefore, the problem is that participants might be annoyed by the number of follow-up questions if they reported using a lot of specific strategies. So they may simply report they had used fewer specific strategies as they got tired with the follow-up questions.

On top of that, I added internalized problems as another outcome variable.

Thus, a simple solution to these concerns is to treat the mediators as dummy variables. This introduces some challenges. First, I need to calculate the indirect effects by specifying new variables that the product of a-paths and b-paths because there is not an off-the-rack indirect effect command for categorical mediators in mplus. Second, doing this requests a lot of computation. It takes more than three hours to run this model with a powerful mac.
